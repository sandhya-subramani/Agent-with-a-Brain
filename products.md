## Known issue: Nordic Oak Standing Desk wobble
Desks shipped before December 2025 can wobble at standing height because of a short
leveling foot. The fix is the free replacement leg kit, part number NL-200, which ships
in 2 days. Offer the kit first. Most customers keep the desk once the kit is installed.
