# XYZ Retail Return Policy
Owner: Customer Experience | Updated: November 2025

- Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Electronics and small appliances (blenders, speakers, headphones) have a 14-day return window.
- Holiday extension: anything delivered between November 1 and December 24 can be returned
  until January 31 of the following year, regardless of category.
- Items marked FINAL SALE cannot be returned or exchanged.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
- Exchanges for the same item in a different size or color are always free.
- A receipt or order number is required. Without one, we offer store credit at the current price.
