# XYZ Retail Shipping FAQ

**How long does shipping take?** Standard shipping is 3 to 5 business days. Express is 1 to 2.

**Is shipping free?** Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Express is a flat $12.

**My package says delivered but I don't have it.** Ask the customer to check with neighbors
and building staff first. If it is still missing after 3 days, we reship at no cost or refund.

**My package is lost in transit.** If tracking has not moved for 10 days, we reship or refund.

**My item arrived damaged.** Ask for a photo, then offer a replacement or a full refund.
No need to ship the damaged item back.

**Can I change my address after ordering?** Only before the order ships. After that, the
carrier has to redirect it, which we cannot guarantee.
